About DBQ Help

Experienced professionals dedicated to serving our nation's veterans

Our Story

DBQ Help is a service of Greene Psychology Group, Inc. and California Psychological, Inc., founded with a clear mission: to ensure every veteran receives thorough, respectful, and professionally conducted mental health evaluations for their VA disability claims.

We understand that navigating the VA disability claims process can be overwhelming. That's why we've built a team of licensed psychologists and legal professionals who specialize in veteran evaluations. Our goal is simple — help you get the benefits you've earned through your service to our country.

Every evaluation we conduct is performed with the same care and thoroughness we would want for our own family members. We believe veterans deserve nothing less.

Our Team

Dr. Eric Greene

CEO & Licensed Psychologist

Licensed Psychologist PsyD Greene Psychology Group California Psychological

Dr. Eric Greene is the founder and CEO of DBQ Help, Greene Psychology Group, Inc., and California Psychological, Inc. As a licensed psychologist, Dr. Greene brings extensive experience in mental health evaluations specifically designed to support veteran disability claims.

Dr. Greene's approach emphasizes thoroughness, compassion, and clarity. He understands that the evaluation process can be stressful for veterans, and he works to create a comfortable, respectful environment while ensuring every detail is properly documented for the VA.

Under Dr. Greene's leadership, DBQ Help has grown to serve veterans across the country, providing the professional evaluations they need to secure their rightfully earned benefits.

Kay Kensington

Legal Director

Legal Director Veteran Services Claims Compliance

Kay Kensington serves as the Legal Director of DBQ Help, overseeing the legal framework that ensures every evaluation and document we produce meets the rigorous standards required by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

Kay's expertise in veteran benefit law and claims compliance provides veterans with confidence that their documentation will be accepted and properly processed. She works closely with Dr. Greene and our psychology team to bridge the gap between clinical evaluation and legal requirements.

Her commitment to veterans' rights and meticulous attention to legal detail make her an invaluable part of the DBQ Help team.
