Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our veteran evaluation services

Questions and Answers

What is a DBQ (Disability Benefits Questionnaire)?

A DBQ is a standardized medical form used by the Department of Veterans Affairs to evaluate disability claims. It captures specific, condition-related information that the VA needs to determine your disability rating.

Think of it as a detailed medical report written in the specific format the VA requires. When completed by a qualified, licensed professional, it gives the VA exactly the information they need to process your claim efficiently.

Who can complete a DBQ?

A DBQ can be completed by any licensed medical professional qualified to evaluate your condition. For mental health DBQs, this means a licensed psychologist or psychiatrist.

At DBQ Help, all evaluations are conducted by licensed psychologists who specialize in veteran mental health evaluations. This specialization means they understand both the clinical and VA-specific requirements of the evaluation.

What is a nexus letter?

A nexus letter is a professional medical opinion that establishes a connection (or "nexus") between your military service and your current medical condition. It provides the VA with evidence that your condition is "at least as likely as not" related to your service.

Nexus letters are often critical to successful claims because they provide the professional medical reasoning the VA needs to approve service-connection.

How long does a DBQ evaluation take?

A typical mental health DBQ evaluation takes approximately 1–2 hours, depending on the complexity of your condition and the records available for review.

Our psychologists also spend additional time before the evaluation reviewing your service records, medical records, and any prior assessments. This preparation ensures your evaluation is thorough and well-documented.

Do I need a referral to get a private DBQ evaluation?

No. Veterans have the right to obtain a private DBQ evaluation from any qualified provider without a referral. You can contact us directly to schedule your evaluation.

What conditions do you evaluate?

We specialize in mental health evaluations, including:

  • Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
  • Major Depressive Disorder
  • Generalized Anxiety Disorder
  • Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) effects
  • Other service-connected mental health conditions

If you're unsure whether your condition qualifies, please call us. We're happy to discuss your situation.

Will the VA accept a private DBQ?

Yes. The VA accepts DBQs completed by qualified private medical providers. Your private DBQ becomes part of your claim file and is considered alongside any VA-conducted evaluations.

Our documentation meets all VA standards because our evaluations are conducted by licensed psychologists who understand the specific requirements.

How do I submit my DBQ to the VA?

You can submit your completed DBQ to the VA in several ways:

  • Upload through your VA.gov account
  • Submit through your Veterans Service Organization (VSO)
  • Mail to your regional VA office
  • Submit through an accredited claims agent or attorney

We can provide guidance on the submission process when we deliver your completed evaluation.

What should I bring to my evaluation?

While not all of these are required, bringing the following can help make your evaluation more thorough:

  • Military service records
  • Previous VA evaluation results
  • Medical records related to your condition
  • Any existing VA rating decisions
  • Personal statements about your condition
Do you offer remote/virtual evaluations?

Please contact us to discuss evaluation options. We serve veterans across the country and can discuss the best approach for your situation.
